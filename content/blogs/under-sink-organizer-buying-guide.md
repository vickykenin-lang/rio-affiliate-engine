## Why Under-Sink Storage Matters in Indian Homes

Indian kitchens and bathrooms often face space constraints, especially in compact apartments and older homes. Under-sink cabinets are frequently underutilized due to poor access, awkward shapes, or lack of proper organizers. A well-chosen under-sink organizer can turn wasted space into functional storage for cleaning supplies, pipes, and spare essentials.

This guide focuses on **rigid, ready-to-assemble, no-drill solutions** suitable for Indian tile, marble, or cement surfaces — prioritizing stability, BPA-free materials, and easy cleaning.

## When to Choose a Fixed vs Rolling Organizer

| Scenario | Recommended type |
|----------|------------------|
| Permanent kitchen installation | Fixed (wolpin-style) |
| Multi-room cleaning cart | Rolling (Amazon Basics) |
| Rental property, no-drill policy | Fixed with anti-slip feet |
| High-mobility workspace (e.g., contractor van) | Rolling |
