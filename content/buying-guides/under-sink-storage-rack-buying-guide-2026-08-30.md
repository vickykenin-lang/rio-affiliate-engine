## How to choose the right rack for your Indian kitchen

1. **Measure your cabinet depth** — standard Indian cabinets are 450–500 mm. Avoid racks >500 mm unless your cabinet is deep.
2. **Check door clearance** — ensure the rack doesn’t block cabinet door opening.
3. **Match load to use** — light storage (bottles, sprays) → 20–30 kg; heavy (pots, detergent jugs) → 50+ kg.
4. **Humidity matters** — coastal or high-rainfall areas → prefer stainless steel (304) or double-coated racks.
5. **Installation ease** — renters or DIYers → tool-free models; permanent fit-outs → screw-fixed.
