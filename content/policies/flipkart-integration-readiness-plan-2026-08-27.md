## 1. Why Flipkart?

- **Strategic diversification:** Reduces merchant concentration risk beyond Amazon Associates India.
- **Audience alignment:** Flipkart hosts many Indian interior-design and home-furniture sellers; verified 3-tier rolling cart equivalent exists in same category.
- **Economic fit:** Competitive commission structure for home & living products; potential for higher volume in Tier 2/3 Indian cities.
- **Platform maturity:** Flipkart Affiliate Network is well-established, with API access and reporting tools.
