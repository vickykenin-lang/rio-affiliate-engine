## How to Integrate This Cart Safely

### ✅ Do
- Place on hard, flat surfaces (tile, marble, polished concrete) for smooth rolling.
- Use the lock castors during sketching, measuring, or client meetings to prevent drift.
- Load heavier items on the bottom shelf to maintain center of gravity.
- Wipe weekly with a dry microfiber cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners on the powder coat.

### ❌ Don’t
- Use on carpet, uneven flooring, or outdoor exposed areas (rust risk in monsoon humidity).
- Exceed 15 kg per shelf (verified limit); overloading can bend shelves or jam castors.
- Store liquids directly on shelves; use sealed containers to prevent spills and corrosion.
