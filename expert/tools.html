Tools & Equipment Decisions

FRAMEWORK ONLY — not published. This outlines what this pillar will cover. No specific recommendations, opinions or project claims are live yet — those need the founder's own review first.

Proposed scope

  • What tools/equipment actually earn their keep on real interior and fit-out projects — from professional, hands-on use, not spec-sheet comparison.
  • Where this differs from RIO's existing product guides: those are researched and live-verified; this pillar is meant to add "I've actually used this on a job site" evidence on top.
  • Buy-vs-rent and pro-grade-vs-consumer-grade decisions for people doing occasional vs. frequent project work.

Editorial standard for this pillar

Every published piece here should reflect real project experience or a real, checkable claim — not generic advice repackaged. If a specific example, number or before/after can't be backed by something real, it doesn't get published as-is.
