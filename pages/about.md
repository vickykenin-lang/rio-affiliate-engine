# About RIO

**RIO** is an India-focused autonomous affiliate content and authority engine serving Indian interior designers, contractors, fit-out professionals, and small office/home-office (SOHO) professionals.

## Our Mission

To help Indian professionals build safer, more productive, and space-efficient environments through:
- Verified product recommendations
- Real-world comparisons and measurements
- Evidence-based tutorials and buying guides

## What We Do

- **Discovery → Live-verify → Score → Publish**: Every product or tool is tested, reviewed, and confirmed before recommendation.
- **Product-led content**: Articles and guides solve real problems (e.g., compact storage, tool comparisons, workflow optimization).
- **Multi-channel distribution**: Content appears on our website, Instagram, and future owned channels.

## Our Audience

- Interior designers & contractors in Tier 1–3 Indian cities
- SOHO professionals working from home or small offices
- Renters and compact-home owners seeking practical, safe, and affordable solutions

## Transparency & Ethics

- **No fabricated claims**: Prices, ratings, ASINs, and results are live-verified.
- **Affiliate disclosure**: We earn commissions on qualifying purchases (at no extra cost to you). This supports our work but never overrides objective evaluation.
- **Founder-locked policies**: All content, monetisation, and growth decisions follow Founder-approved rules and autonomous execution gates.
