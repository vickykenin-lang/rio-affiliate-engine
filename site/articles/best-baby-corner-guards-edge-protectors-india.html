Best Baby Corner Guards and Edge Protectors for Indian Homes

Once a baby starts pulling up on furniture, sharp table and cabinet edges become the most common source of small household injuries. Corner and edge guards are the cheapest, fastest first step in baby-proofing a home — but adhesive quality and material softness vary a lot between listings that look identical in photos.

What to check before buying

  • Confirm the adhesive is rated for your surface (wood, laminate, glass) and check reviews for real-world adhesion complaints, not just the marketing copy.
  • Look for non-toxic, BPA-free material specifically called out — this matters more for corner guards than most baby products since toddlers mouth them.
  • Check whether the pack size actually covers your home's edge count before buying — most tables/desks need 4 corner pieces at minimum, plus strip protectors for long edges.
