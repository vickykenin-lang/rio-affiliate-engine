Best Cabinet and Drawer Safety Locks for Childproofing Indian Homes

Kitchen and bathroom cabinets are where the genuinely dangerous stuff lives — cleaning chemicals, knives, medicines — so locks here matter more than almost any other baby-proofing purchase. Adhesive locks are the standard no-drill option for Indian homes, especially rented ones, but adhesive quality and one-hand operability vary a lot between listings.

What to check before buying

  • Confirm it's a genuinely one-hand-operable design if you'll often have a baby in your other arm — some cheaper locks need two hands, which defeats the point.
  • Check the adhesive is rated 3M or equivalent and read reviews for real durability over months, not just initial install feedback.
  • Count how many locks you actually need — kitchen + bathroom cabinets and drawers in a typical Indian flat usually need more than a starter 4-6 pack.
