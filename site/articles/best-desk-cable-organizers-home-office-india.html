Best Desk and Cable Organizers for Small Home Offices in India

A cramped work-from-home corner gets messier fast once a laptop, extension board, router and charger cables pile up. An under-desk tray keeps cables off the floor and off the desktop without needing extra furniture.

What to check before buying

  • Measure the underside of your desk — check clearance for screws or clamps.
  • Confirm the mounting method (screw-fixed vs clamp) matches your desk's edge thickness.
  • Check the tray length against how many cables/extension boards you actually need to hold.
