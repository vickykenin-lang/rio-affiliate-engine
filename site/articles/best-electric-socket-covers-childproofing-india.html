Best Electric Socket Covers for Childproofing Indian Homes

Indian homes typically have sockets at low, easy-to-reach heights for crawling babies and toddlers, and most Indian wiring uses a mix of 5A and 15A points in the same room. A cover set that only fits one socket type leaves gaps — the most common mistake is buying a pack sized for one country's socket standard without checking it covers both amperages you actually have.

What to check before buying

  • Confirm the pack includes covers for both 5A and 15A sockets if your home has both — most Indian flats do.
  • Check it comes with a removal tool/pin — covers with no easy adult-removal method become an annoyance you stop using.
  • Read reviews for durability specifically, since this product type sees the most on/off cycles of any baby-proofing item and cheap plastic can crack over repeated use.
