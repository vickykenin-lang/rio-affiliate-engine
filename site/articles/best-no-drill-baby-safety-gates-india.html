Best No-Drill Baby Safety Gates for Rented Homes in India

Stairs and kitchen doorways are the highest-risk spots for a mobile toddler, and most Indian renters can't drill into walls or door frames to fit a permanent gate. Pressure-mounted, no-drill gates solve this — the tradeoff is that fit and installation quality matter more than with a screwed-in gate, so a wrong-size or loose-fit gate is a real safety risk, not just an inconvenience.

What to check before buying

  • Measure your doorway or stair opening precisely and check the gate's adjustable range covers it with margin — a gate stretched to its maximum width is less secure.
  • Confirm the mounting method is genuinely pressure-mounted/no-drill if you're renting, not just marketed as "easy install" while still needing screws.
  • Check reviews specifically for fit and extension-piece complaints if you need an extension kit for a wider opening — this is the most common real-world failure point for adjustable gates.
