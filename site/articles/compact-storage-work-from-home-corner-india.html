Compact Storage Ideas for a Work-From-Home Corner

Most Indian WFH corners aren't a dedicated room — they're a desk carved out of a bedroom or living room. The two things that make that corner feel chaotic fastest are cable clutter and paperwork with no fixed home. Fix those two before buying any new furniture.

What to check before buying anything

  • Trace every cable currently on your desk or floor — most WFH clutter is cables, not stuff.
  • Decide what actually needs to live at the desk vs. what can move to a drawer elsewhere.
  • Measure the desk's underside and edge thickness before buying a clamp-on or screw-fixed tray.
