Under-Sink Storage Ideas for Indian Kitchens

Before buying anything, empty the cabinet and map where the plumbing actually sits — most under-sink space gets wasted because people buy a rack sized for an empty cabinet, then find it doesn't clear the trap or the shut-off valve. Zone the space first: cleaning supplies on one side, bins or a pull-out on the other, and keep the area directly under the pipe joint empty in case of leaks.

What to check before buying anything

  • Measure width, depth and the height from the cabinet floor to the lowest pipe joint — this is the number that actually constrains what fits.
  • Decide whether you need a fixed rack or something removable for full cabinet cleaning, since under-sink areas need more frequent cleaning than most cabinets.
  • Keep anything you'd be upset to lose to a slow leak (documents, electronics) out of this cabinet entirely — rack quality doesn't fix a plumbing risk.
