## Why under-sink storage matters for Indian kitchens and workspaces

Under-sink space is often wasted in Indian homes and small offices. With compact kitchens, fit-out budgets, and tight timelines, professionals need storage that is:

- **Space-efficient** — fits standard 15"–18" sink cabinets
- **Durable** — resists moisture and daily use
- **Easy to install** — no drilling or permanent modification
- **Affordable** — ₹500–₹1,500 price band for most professionals

This guide helps you choose the right under-sink storage rack for your project.

## Key selection criteria

### 1. Cabinet dimensions
Most Indian kitchen under-sink cabinets are 15"–18" wide and 22"–24" deep. Measure before buying.

### 2. Material
| Material | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|----------|------|------|----------|
| **Plastic (PP/PE)** | Rust-proof, lightweight, ₹500–₹1,200 | Less load capacity (5–10 kg) | Wet areas, renters |
| **Stainless steel** | High load (15–25 kg), modern look | Expensive (₹1,500–₹3,000), can dent | Commercial kitchens |
| **Iron with powder coat** | Strong, mid-price (₹900–₹1,800) | Risk of rust if coating chips | Dry storage zones |

### 3. Tier count & adjustability
- **1-tier**: Single shelf, max height clearance (good for tall bottles)
- **2-tier**: Balanced height split, most versatile
- **3-tier**: Maximum storage, but lower individual shelf height

Adjustable shelves are highly recommended for mixed bottle/jar sizes.

### 4. Load capacity
- **Light duty (≤5 kg)**: Spices, small bottles
- **Medium duty (5–10 kg)**: Detergents, cleaning supplies
- **Heavy duty (10–25 kg)**: Bulk packs, large jugs

## How to install (no tools needed)
1. Measure cabinet width at floor level.
2. Place rack inside; adjust expandable legs if present.
3. Test stability by pressing down on each corner.
4. Load gradually: start with light items, then heavier bottles.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- ❌ Buying based on photos alone — always check dimensions.
- ❌ Overloading plastic racks — 10 kg is the practical limit for most.
- ❌ Ignoring return policy — 30-day returns are standard on Amazon.
