## How to choose the right under-sink organizer for your home

### Step 1: Measure your under-sink gap

Use a tape measure to record:
- **Width:** Left wall to right wall (inside the cabinet)
- **Depth:** Front edge to back wall (including pipe clearance)
- **Height:** Floor to underside of countertop
- **Obstructions:** Note pipe locations, electrical outlets, or drain traps

> **Pro tip:** Indian modular kitchens often have 30–35 cm width and 25–30 cm depth. If your gap is narrower, prioritize adjustable or slim-profile organizers.

### Step 2: Match your use case

| Use Case | Recommended Type | Why |

| Wet-zone storage (bathroom/kitchen sink) | Wolpin or Amazon Basics rolling rack | Rustproof + moisture-resistant |
