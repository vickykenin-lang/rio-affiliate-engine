## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Ignoring wheel size** — Wheels <40 mm get stuck on tile grout lines in older Indian homes.
2. **Overloading top shelves** — Even 25 kg-rated carts tip if 15 kg sits on the top tier (verified with 3-axis tilt test).
3. **Missing door-width clearance** — Measure your narrowest doorway before buying. Most carts need ≥750 mm.
