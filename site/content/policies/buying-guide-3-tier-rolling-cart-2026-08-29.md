## How to Choose the Right 3-Tier Rolling Cart

### 1. Intended Use
- **Interior designers / stylists:** Prioritise lightweight, quiet wheels and aesthetic finish (e.g., matte black, oak veneer).
- **Contractors / fit-out teams:** Prioritise load capacity, rust resistance, and corner protection.
- **Small office / home office:** Prioritise compact footprint, cable management slots, and modern appearance.

### 2. Critical Specs Checklist
- **Weight capacity:** Multiply per-shelf rating by 3 for total. For tool carts, aim for ≥30 kg total.
- **Wheel type:** Swivel + lockable wheels prevent accidental movement on tile or marble floors.
- **Edge protection:** Rounded or rubber-edged shelves reduce chipping and injury risk.
- **Adjustability:** Shelf-height adjustability allows stacking taller items on lower tiers.

### 3. India-Specific Considerations
- **Humidity resistance:** Powder-coated steel outperforms bare iron in humid coastal cities.
- **Doorway fit:** Standard Indian door width is ~30–32 inches (76–81 cm); verify cart width ≤70 cm.
- **Voltage compatibility:** Not applicable for manual carts, but verify if cart includes lighting or power tools.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Assuming all "rolling carts" are equal** — many listings omit load ratings or wheel-lock details.
- **Ignoring assembly time** — some carts require 30+ minutes and two people; look for tool-free designs.
- **Overlooking warranty terms** — Indian seller warranty ≠ manufacturer warranty; confirm serviceability in your city.
