Editorial Policy

RIO uses a problem-first approach: define the space constraint, determine measurable requirements, identify relevant product types, verify product-specific facts, and only then activate merchant links.

What we check

  • Dimensions and required clearances.
  • Mounting method and stated load limits where relevant.
  • Material and maintenance considerations.
  • Merchant availability and current product specifications before linking.
  • Affiliate disclosure placement before an affiliate link.

What commission does not control

Commission availability does not by itself determine whether a product is recommended or where it is ranked.

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected when identified. Time-sensitive product facts should be refreshed before publication or when an article is materially updated.
